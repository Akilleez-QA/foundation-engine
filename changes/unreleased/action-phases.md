- **Optional action phases** (`createActionPhases` in `@kits/capabilities`): creator-authored windows and once-only marks over one action's own position. Delivery is exactly once across any step size; claims are once per range; handover suppression and validated restore are provided for rollback. It is a pure helper with no clock, effect or persistence owner. See [ADR 0097](docs/adr/0097-action-phase-windows.md).
