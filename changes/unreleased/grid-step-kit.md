- **Optional grid-step kit** (`@kits/grid-step`): tile-to-tile actor movement with facing and turn-before-move. Refused moves are classified (leash, impassable, directional blocks, tile classes, elevation, occupied). It also covers ledge jumps, conveyor and ice tiles, tile reservations while moving, follower lines, a handheld-style preset and validated snapshots. See [ADR 0150](docs/adr/0150-grid-step-movement.md).
