- **Optional contact kit** (`@kits/contact`): vertical-cylinder, sphere and box contact volumes with layer and mask bits and exact overlap tests. Each update reports deterministic enter, stay and exit events. Pair and per-body bounds give existing contacts priority. Intangible or removed bodies produce exits, and snapshots support saves and rollback. See [ADR 0151](docs/adr/0151-contact-layer.md).
