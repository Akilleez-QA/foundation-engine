- **Optional population kit (`@kits/population`, requires `spatial`):** authored placements that spawn near observers with hysteresis and bounded per-update work, remember destroyed placements (`never` depletion persisted through a strict save section, `visit`, `leave`), and update tiers that run near entities every step, freeze far ones or round-robin them with conserved time (steps bounded by a catch-up limit). No owner is installed. See [the guide](docs/guides/population.md) and [ADR 0092](docs/adr/0092-population-placements-and-tiers.md).
