- **Optional data-defined formulas.** Optional `formulas` kit: data-defined stat and damage expressions (JSON or text), ordered formula sheets, declared modifier stacking stages and a damage model with a fixed resolution order (miss, stacking, resistance/immunity, rounding, floors). Deterministic arithmetic, randomness only from `ctx.random`-style sources, bounded work. Generic presets plus a named transcription of one game's documented damage arithmetic. See [ADR 0124](docs/adr/0124-data-defined-formulas.md).
