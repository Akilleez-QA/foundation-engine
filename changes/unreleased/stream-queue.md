- **Optional streaming queue.** Optional streaming queue (`@kits/streaming`): rank play-time load requests (model assets and the clips they carry, records, sounds) under concurrency and byte budgets, with shared requests, cancellation that holds slots until loads settle, deterministic retry backoff and optional preemption, through ports onto the existing lease caches and model owner. See [ADR 0130](docs/adr/0130-optional-streaming-queue.md).
