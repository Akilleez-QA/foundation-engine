- **Optional volume queries:** `@kits/volume-query` checks sphere and capsule bodies against an immutable snapshot of static spheres, capsules and oriented boxes: overlap, fixed-orientation sweep with a conservative first contact, and capsule headroom. Creator-configured evaluation and iteration ceilings refuse rather than claim clear. No physics world, controller or dependency. See [ADR 0098](docs/adr/0098-bounded-volume-queries.md).
